Chapter 33: Bedtime Stories

"The Mad Hunter"
March 25, 2020 — From the creations inspired by Alice in Wonderland Mad Hatters & "The Rages Band"

Once upon a time, there was a boastful man who knew no limits in eating food. His name was Buddy, but his gluttony slowly reduced him to impoverishment. He desired to consume unlimited quantities of food, yet his hunger was always accompanied by intense rage. He turned mad because nothing was ever left for him after eating—fruits, meats, and vegetables vanished in his endless cravings.

Driven by madness, he decided to chase outside the town of Bachelor’s House. In his mindless wanderings, he stumbled upon a gun. Thus began the tale of "The Mad Hunter". Strange and forbidden thoughts took hold of his brain. Though God's commandment clearly warned "You shall not kill", Buddy began a pure killing spree—slaughtering and shooting fruits, vegetables, and meats to satisfy his addiction.

One year passed long ago. One day, while consumed by his madness and taking shots at wild roaming food creatures, Buddy fired a shot without looking. He didn't notice that the bullet struck a young walking boy named Alito.

Wounded and bleeding, Alito collapsed. Buddy apologized to himself, realizing the horrifying consequences of his strange thoughts and food addiction. Unable to meet Alito's eyes in fear of being a murderer, Buddy fled into the wilderness before the Police Chief could take him to the precinct jail.

Moral Lesson

"Don't overcome pressure to eat. Be contented to eat in other days. Or else the punishment by God is punished by you too, for you shall not kill. Be contented with what you eat rather than consuming pure strange desires. You need conscience first before you eat or do anything."
